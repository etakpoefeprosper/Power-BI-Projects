##  Project Overview

The **Netflix Movie Performance Analysis Dashboard** provides a comprehensive evaluation of movie budgets, box office outcomes, and ratings. The dashboard was designed to uncover financial and performance trends across genres and countries.

### Objective of the Analysis:

✔ Evaluate overall investment performance  
✔ Analyse budget distribution  
✔ Identify genre-level spending patterns  
✔ Examine country-level budget allocation  
✔ Assess average movie ratings  

---

##  Executive Summary

The dashboard reveals strong financial indicators:

- **Total Budget:** **$823.4M**
- **Total Box Office:** **$1.5bn**
- **Average Movie Rating:** **6.8**

The analysis indicates that investment strategies vary across genres and countries, with Action and Drama films receiving the highest financial allocation.

##  Analytical Observations

1. Budget concentration is highest in **Action and Drama genres**.
2. Revenue significantly exceeds budget, indicating **positive return on investment (ROI)**.
3. Country-level budget allocation suggests **diversified production strategy**.
4. Ratings remain stable but not exceptional.
5. Lower-budget genres may offer opportunities for cost-efficient growth.

---

##  Key Insights

1. **Action movies represent the largest financial investment**, highlighting Netflix’s focus on high-engagement content.
2. **Total Box Office ($1.5bn) substantially exceeds Total Budget**, indicating strong financial performance.
3. **Germany and Canada lead budget allocation**, suggesting strategic production investments.
4. **Average ratings (6.8) suggest consistent but improvable content reception**.
5. **Budget disparities across genres reveal prioritization strategy**, potentially aligned with viewer demand.
