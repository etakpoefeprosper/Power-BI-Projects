##  Project Overview

The **BPS Business Performance Dashboard** provides a comprehensive analysis of organizational sales, profit, discount behaviour, and product performance. The dashboard was designed to transform raw business data into an interactive decision-support tool.

The objective of this analysis is to:

✔ Evaluate overall financial performance  
✔ Identify profitability drivers  
✔ Understand discount patterns  
✔ Assess segment & product contributions  

---

##  Executive Summary

The business demonstrates strong financial performance:

- **Total Sales:** **59.89M**
- **Total Profit:** **7.71M**
- **Profit Margin:** **0.13 (13%)**

Sales growth and profit generation indicate a healthy business model, although discount distribution and segment contributions reveal opportunities for optimization.

---

##  Tools & Technologies

This dashboard was developed using:

- **Microsoft Excel** → Data preparation & modelling  
- **Power BI** → Data visualization & dashboard design  

Excel supported structured data cleaning, while Power BI enabled interactive storytelling through visuals and filters.

##  Key Insights

1. **Strong revenue performance**, driven primarily by Small Business customers.
2. **Profit margin remains stable**, suggesting effective cost management.
3. **Discount-heavy sales strategy**, indicating reliance on pricing incentives.
4. **Sales concentration across products**, highlighting portfolio strengths.
5. **Segment imbalance**, with smaller segments underperforming.

---

##  Business Recommendations

###  1. Discount Optimization
- Evaluate discount impact on profit margins
- Reduce excessive discounting where possible

###  2. Segment Growth Strategy
- Strengthen Midmarket & Channel Partner engagement
- Develop targeted sales campaigns

###  3. Product Strategy Enhancement
- Promote lower-performing products
- Analyze product demand drivers

###  4. Profitability Protection
- Maintain margin discipline
- Optimize pricing strategies

###  5. Performance Monitoring
- Track sales & discount trends continuously
- Use dashboards for proactive decisions

---

##  Conclusion

The **BPS Business Performance Dashboard** reveals a business with strong sales and profitability metrics supported by an effective product mix. However, discount patterns and segment disparities highlight areas for strategic refinement.

This project demonstrates the power of **Excel for data preparation** and **Power BI for business intelligence visualization**, enabling data-driven decision-making.
