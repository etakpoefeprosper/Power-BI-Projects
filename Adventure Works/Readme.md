##  Project Overview

This project evaluates sales performance for **Adventure Works** across the 2013–2014 period. The dashboard provides an interactive analytical view of products, customers, territories, and order quantities.

The objective of this analysis is to:

✔ Measure sales and order performance  
✔ Identify high-performing products  
✔ Evaluate customer purchasing behaviour  
✔ Analyse geographic sales distribution  
✔ Generate data-driven business insights  

---

##  Executive Summary

The business demonstrates strong commercial activity:

- **Total Products:** **504**
- **Total Customers:** **19.12K**
- **Total Order Quantity:** **275K**

Sales performance is driven primarily by **Mountain Bike products**, **Store customers**, and key territories such as **Southwest** and **Canada**.

##  Key Insights

1. **Mountain Bike products dominate revenue generation**, indicating strong demand for high-value items.
2. **Store customers account for over 78% of orders**, highlighting B2B sales strength.
3. **Southwest and Canada drive major order volumes**, showing geographic concentration.
4. **Road Bikes lead sub-category sales**, suggesting strong preference for performance products.
5. **Accessory categories provide stable support revenue**, indicating cross-selling effectiveness.

##  Business Recommendations

###  1. Expand High-Performing Product Lines
- Increase inventory for Mountain & Road Bikes
- Leverage best-selling variants

###  2. Strengthen Individual Customer Segment
- Develop targeted marketing campaigns
- Introduce personalized promotions

###  3. Geographic Growth Strategy
- Replicate Southwest success strategies
- Invest in emerging territories

###  4. Product Portfolio Optimization
- Promote underperforming categories
- Analyse demand drivers

###  5. Enhance Cross-Selling Opportunities
- Bundle accessories with bike purchases
- Encourage higher basket size

##  Conclusion

The **Adventure Works Sales Analysis** reveals a business with strong product demand, a dominant store-based customer segment, and concentrated regional performance.

This project demonstrates how **SQL for structured data querying** and **Power BI for visualization** can transform transactional data into actionable business intelligence.
